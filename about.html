What is BDCalc?

BDCalc is a free online calculator platform designed specifically for people in Bangladesh. Our tools are built to handle the unique financial, legal, and everyday calculation needs of Bangladeshi citizens — from salary breakdowns under the Bangladesh labour law to land measurements in traditional Bengali units like Katha and Bigha.

All tools on BDCalc are completely free to use. No registration required. No data stored. Just fast, accurate results on any device.

Why We Built BDCalc

Most online calculators are designed for Western markets and do not account for Bangladesh-specific rules — like the National Board of Revenue VAT structure, traditional land units used in property deeds, or the Bangladesh Bank salary disbursement norms.

BDCalc fills this gap by providing tools that reflect actual Bangladeshi standards, updated for 2026.

Accuracy & Updates

Our calculators are based on official Bangladesh government data including:

  • National Board of Revenue (NBR) VAT rates 2025–26
  • Bangladesh Bank lending rate guidelines
  • Traditional Bengali land measurement standards
  • Ministry of Finance salary norms

We review and update our tools regularly to reflect any changes in Bangladesh law or financial regulations.
