How Bangladesh Salary is Calculated in 2026

In Bangladesh, monthly salary consists of Basic Pay, House Rent Allowance (HRA), Medical Allowance, and other allowances. The standard HRA is typically 50% of basic salary for private sector employees.

  • Provident Fund: 10% of basic salary (both employee and employer contribute)
  • Income Tax: Applicable if annual income exceeds BDT 3,50,000 (2025-26 threshold)
  • Festival Bonus: Usually 2 Eid bonuses = 2 months basic salary annually
  • Gratuity: Applicable after 5 years of service

Bangladesh VAT Rates 2025-26

Bangladesh has a standard VAT rate of 15% on most goods and services. The National Board of Revenue (NBR) administers VAT collection under the VAT and SD Act 2012.

  • Standard Rate: 15% on most goods and services
  • Truncated Rate: 10% on selected services
  • Reduced Rate: 7.5% on certain services like restaurants and software
  • Essential Goods: 5% on specific essential commodities
  • Wholesale/Trade: 2% on certain wholesale transactions

Bangladesh Land Measurement Units

Bangladesh uses traditional Bengali land measurement units alongside metric units. Understanding conversions is essential for property dealings and land registration.

  • 1 Bigha = 20 Katha = 1,333.33 Square meters (BD standard)
  • 1 Katha = 720 Square feet = 66.89 Square meters
  • 1 Decimal = 435.6 Square feet = 40.47 Square meters
  • 1 Acre = 100 Decimal = 4,047 Square meters
  • 1 Bigha = 33 Decimal (approximately)

Bank Loan EMI Rates in Bangladesh 2026

Major banks in Bangladesh offer home loans and personal loans at varying interest rates. The Bangladesh Bank sets the policy rate that influences lending rates.

  • Home Loan: 9% – 13% per annum (varies by bank)
  • Personal Loan: 10% – 18% per annum
  • Car Loan: 9% – 15% per annum
  • SME Loan: 7% – 12% per annum
  • EMI = [P × R × (1+R)^N] / [(1+R)^N – 1]
